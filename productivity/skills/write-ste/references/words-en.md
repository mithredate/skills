# English words

This file holds the English words for `write-ste`.

- The short common verbs are do, use, make, start, stop, check, write, read, delete, move, run, open, close, list, show, give, get, keep, and put.
- These words are not allowed: perform, utilize, initiate, terminate, facilitate, leverage, ensure, groom, prior to, in order to. Write "make sure" for ensure, "refine" for groom, "before" for prior to, and "to" for in order to.
- Do not write "should" or "may". Write "must" for a rule and "can" for a possibility.
- The noun form of a verb is its -ing form. Do not use an -ing form as a noun or as a sentence opener.
